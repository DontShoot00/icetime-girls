ICE TIME PLAYBOOK: HOW TO PUT YOUR APP ONLINE (free, about 5 minutes)

1. Unzip this file. You'll get a folder called "icetime-app".
2. On a computer, go to:  https://app.netlify.com/drop
3. Drag the whole "icetime-app" folder onto the page.
4. Netlify gives you a link ending in ".netlify.app". Your app is live.
5. IMPORTANT: Sign up for a free Netlify account and claim the site right away.
   If you don't, Netlify deletes it within about an hour.
6. Optional: in Netlify, go to Site settings > Change site name
   to get a nicer link, like  icetime-hockey.netlify.app

HOW PLAYERS INSTALL IT
- iPhone/iPad: open the link in Safari, tap "Install app" and follow the steps
  (Share button > Add to Home Screen).
- Android: open the link in Chrome and tap "Install app".
- After installing, it opens from the home screen like any app and works
  at the rink with no signal.

UPDATING THE APP LATER
- Ask Claude for the change. You'll get a new folder.
- In Netlify, open your site > Deploys, and drag the new folder onto the
  drop area. Players get the update the next time they open the app with internet.

WHAT'S IN THE FOLDER (don't rename these)
- index.html            the app
- manifest.webmanifest  the app's name, icon and colors
- sw.js                 makes it work offline
- icons/                the home-screen icons
